# Ecommerce Sales Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes over 51,000 ecommerce sales records to uncover valuable business insights related to sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, and growth trends.

The goal is to help businesses make data-driven decisions by identifying profitable categories, loss-making products, and revenue-generating customer segments.

---

## Business Questions

- Which product categories generate the highest profit?
- Which products are causing losses?
- Is the business growing year over year?
- Which customer segments contribute the most revenue?
- Which regions perform best in terms of sales and profit?

---

## Data Cleaning

The following preprocessing steps were performed:

- Checked and handled missing values
- Removed duplicate records
- Verified and corrected data types
- Prepared data for analysis

---

## Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

- Category-wise Sales & Profit Analysis
- Region-wise Performance Analysis
- Yearly Sales Trend Analysis
- Customer Segment Analysis
- Profit vs Discount Analysis
- Correlation Analysis

---

## Key Insights

- Technology is the most profitable product category.
- Consumer segment generates the highest revenue.
- Several products are loss-making due to heavy discounts.
- Sales show consistent year-over-year growth.
- Some regions outperform others in both sales and profitability.
